3D-DASH is the follow-up program to COSMOS-DASH, extending the total area to 1.43 sq. deg. in HST WFC3/F160W, making it the largest survey done by HST in the near-IR. 3D-DASH imaging and related tools are available on the [3D-DASH MAST page](https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/3d-dash).
